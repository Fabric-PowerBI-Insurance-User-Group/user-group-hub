## Mission

FPBIUG connects insurance professionals and data practitioners who want to learn, apply, and share Microsoft Fabric, Power BI, analytics, data engineering, AI, and visualization practices. FPBIUG turns live learning into durable group knowledge while helping participants develop technical confidence, professional relationships, certification readiness, and leadership opportunities.

## Launch Priorities

1. **Momentum:** Establish a dependable monthly rhythm and a visible program pipeline.
2. **Credibility:** Recruit respected experts and practitioners while supporting new voices.
3. **Habit formation:** Make it easy to return, ask questions, join cohorts, and contribute.
4. **User Group progression:** Create paths from attendee to contributor, facilitator, speaker, and champion.
5. **Durable value:** Publish approved recordings and reusable resources for continued learning.

## Program Commitments

- Monthly meetings occur on the **first Tuesday evening** at **6:00 PM Eastern Time**.
- User Group office hours are **Coming Soon**.
- Certification cohorts will be separate, time-bound series held twice weekly for two to three weeks once launched.
- Initial certification areas are **PL-300, DP-600, and DP-700**.
- Special formats may include demos, workshops, panels, expert conversations, and showcases.

## Content Scope

### In Scope

Microsoft Fabric, Power BI, analytics, semantic modeling, DAX, data storytelling, OneLake, Lakehouse, Warehouse, pipelines, notebooks, AI, Copilot, data agents, certification preparation, professional development, and insurance-oriented technology scenarios using safe sample data.

### Outside Scope

Legal or regulatory guidance, compliance interpretation, audit or tax advice, jurisdiction-specific insurance direction, confidential implementation details, exam dumps, restricted exam content, and sales-only presentations.

## Independence and Affiliations

FPBIUG is group-led. References to Microsoft products describe the technology focus and do not by themselves imply Microsoft ownership, sponsorship, endorsement, or funding. Speakers should disclose relevant employer, product, or commercial affiliations.
