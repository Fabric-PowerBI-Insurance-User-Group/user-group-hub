# FPBIUG Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

The Fabric & Power BI Insurance User Group is committed to a welcoming, respectful, inclusive, and harassment-free experience across meetings, study groups, online discussions, GitHub, social channels, and user group events.

## Expected Conduct

- Communicate constructively and welcome different experience levels.
- Protect privacy, confidential information, and intellectual property.
- Credit sources and disclose relevant affiliations.
- Keep participation relevant to learning, technology, and user group outcomes.
- Follow organizer direction during live and online experiences.

## Unacceptable Conduct

Harassment, intimidation, discriminatory conduct, personal attacks, repeated disruption, unauthorized promotion, misuse of participant data, or deliberate publication of confidential information is not permitted.

## Reporting

The public contact email is **To be determined**. Until a public mailbox is established, concerns should be reported privately to a user group co-owner through an established organizer channel. Sensitive conduct reports must not be submitted as public GitHub issues.

## Organizer Response

Organizers may moderate or remove content, pause participation, or take other proportionate action to protect participants and preserve the integrity of FPBIUG.
